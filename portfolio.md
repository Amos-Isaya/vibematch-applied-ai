# VibeMatch: Verified Music Recommendations

**Code:** https://github.com/Amos-Isaya/vibematch-applied-ai

VibeMatch extends my Module 3 music recommender into a small, explainable recommendation workflow. It retrieves songs that match a listener's genre or mood, checks their energy against the request, and ranks the candidates with reasons. A listener can review the suggestions and revise their preferences when no song passes the energy guardrail. The catalog is fictional and the system is rule-based, so its scores are not predictions of enjoyment.

**Portfolio reflection — draft to personalize:** This project represents my interest in building AI systems that people can inspect and question. I explored how a recommendation can look plausible while missing a user's constraint, and how verification, tests, and clear explanations make that limitation visible. I would next test the design with listeners and a more representative catalog before making claims about recommendation quality.
